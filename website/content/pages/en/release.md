---
title: "Release status"
description: "HeartPro availability and supported platforms."
---

HeartPro is currently available through TestFlight testing. A public App Store download link is not yet available.

Version 0.6.0 includes ECG history, daily heart summaries, trends, waveform inspection, automatic research estimates, notes and PDF reports. The app supports English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Korean, German and Spanish; this website currently supports English and Simplified Chinese.

HeartPro is operated under the Alkinum brand. Public privacy and terms pages are available. Automatic estimates have not undergone independent accuracy validation on real Apple Watch recordings. Synthetic examples and software tests do not demonstrate clinical accuracy.

Read the [guide](/docs), [methods and limits](/docs/methods), or [help](/support).

## Intended use
HeartPro organizes existing Apple Health records and provides local, informational analysis, notes and PDF export. It does not measure a new ECG, monitor you continuously, provide emergency alerts, diagnose disease or recommend treatment. Automatic measurements and candidate findings are not clinically validated. A score, missing flag or normal-looking waveform does not establish safety. Consult a doctor before making medical decisions.

## Requirements and availability
Use requires iOS/iPadOS 18 or later. Personal-data features depend on authorized records available in Apple Health on that device. Recording ECGs requires compatible Apple hardware and regional eligibility. Demo mode is available without Health access. Apple does not endorse HeartPro, and its ECG clearance does not validate HeartPro's algorithms.

## Your records and reports
You control permissions and sharing. Keep copies of notes you need before clearing data or deleting the app; HeartPro does not back up notes to a developer server. Do not use the app to process another person's data without the necessary permission. See the [privacy policy](/privacy) and [methods](/docs/methods).

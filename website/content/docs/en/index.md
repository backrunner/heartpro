On first launch, read and acknowledge the dedicated usage notice before the guide or Health authorization. HeartPro’s analysis is not medical advice and cannot replace clinicians or medical services. Seek prompt care if you feel unwell and contact local emergency services in an emergency.


## Connect your records
Read or close the first-use guide. Select **Connect Apple Health**, then choose whether to allow ECG, heart-rate and HRV reads. Records must already be available in Health on this device. HeartPro cannot record an ECG or supply missing readings.

You can also select **Try examples first** without sharing health data. Every example is clearly labelled synthetic and cannot be saved to Apple Health.

## Explore a day
The ECG tab opens history and a daily overview. Open the overview for daily summaries or switch to trends by day, week, month or year. The calendar filters past ECGs by date. A gap indicates missing data, not zero and not absence of a health issue.

## Open an ECG
Tap a record to view its original waveform, source classification, measurements and notes. Scroll horizontally to review the full recording. Zoom preserves the time/voltage scale ratio. The expand control opens the waveform full screen; the ruler lets you select two points to compare time and voltage. Displayed screen sizes are not physical paper measurements.

Automatic annotations and measurements are estimates. See [methods and limitations](/docs/methods) before interpreting them. Apple's stored classification is distinct from HeartPro's own calculations.

## Keep context and export
Use the note button to record context or symptoms. Saving symptoms to Health is optional and creates separate entries. Generate a PDF with the share button, then choose where to send it. Your notes may appear in that report.

Manage [data and permissions](/docs/data), or [get support](/support).

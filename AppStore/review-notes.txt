REVIEW WITHOUT A WATCH OR HEALTH RECORDS
1. On first launch, read the usage notice and tap “I understand, continue”. Read or dismiss the usage guide.
2. Select “Try examples first”. Synthetic examples are labelled in the app, generated locally and never written to Apple Health. This is the same example mode available to every user.
3. In the ECG tab, open the daily overview to see summaries and trends. Return to history and open a recording to inspect its waveform, zoom, two-point measurement and full-screen view.
4. Scroll through the recording details to review interval/segment estimates, short-recording HRV, premature-beat candidates and the rhythm reference score. Information buttons explain methods, missing values and limitations. Apple’s stored classification and synthetic classifications are identified by source.
5. Add a note and choose a context item. In example mode, notes stay in memory and Health writes are disabled.
6. Tap the share icon to generate a PDF; tap the share icon again to choose a destination in the system share sheet.
7. Settings → Usage notice reopens the first-use notice. Settings also contains Help, Health access, analysis limitations, privacy links and local-data controls.

HEALTH ACCESS
“Connect Apple Health” requests read access to ECGs, heart rate and SDNN. HeartPro does not record ECGs. New ECG recordings require a compatible Apple Watch and regional ECG availability. On iPad, records must be available in Health on that iPad. HealthKit does not disclose whether read permission was denied; an empty result is not proof of authorization.

Write access is requested only when a user chooses to save supported symptoms. These become separate HeartPro entries in Health; original ECGs are read-only. Denied writes preserve the local note. Synthetic data and automated findings are never written to Health. Clearing local data removes local records, notes and temporary PDFs, but not original Health records, independent symptoms already saved there or files shared elsewhere.

DATA AND ANALYSIS
Health records, notes and analysis are processed on device, with protected local storage and no HeartPro iCloud synchronization. PDFs are shared only at the user's direction. No health-record uploads, remote AI or developer telemetry are implemented.

Local deterministic methods estimate peaks, intervals, waveform segments, short-recording HRV and premature-beat candidates. The rhythm reference score is the rounded fraction of reviewable beats not flagged by the rules; it is not a health score or risk probability. General reference values do not produce personalized disease, medication or treatment advice. No diagnosis, continuous monitoring or emergency alerts are provided. The usage notice is shown before record loading or Health access, to all users.

Independent accuracy validation on real Apple Watch recordings has not been completed. Synthetic tests and public Holter benchmarks do not establish Watch accuracy. No clinical validation, regulatory clearance or Apple endorsement is claimed.

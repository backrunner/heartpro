HeartPro - iPhone and iPad, iOS/iPadOS 18+
Bundle ID: com.alkinum.heartpro

PURPOSE AND LIMITATIONS
An informational measurement and record-review tool, not intended for use as a medical device. HeartPro displays existing Apple Health records and local derived statistics. It offers no diagnosis, treatment recommendations, normal reference ranges, continuous monitoring or emergency alerts. Estimates can be wrong. No clinical validation, regulatory clearance or Apple endorsement is claimed. Consult a doctor before medical decisions.

ALL FUNCTIONS REMAIN DISCLOSED
The app estimates peaks, intervals, waveform segments, short-recording HRV, premature-beat candidates and a rhythm reference score. The score is the proportion of reviewable beats not flagged by HeartPro's own rules, not a medical scale or health/risk score. Candidate labels do not establish a disease or atrial/ventricular origin. Only within-record distributions are displayed. Missing measurements remain blank. Apple's stored classification is identified separately.

REVIEW WITHOUT HEALTH RECORDS
1. Read the first-use notice and the operational guide, then select the synthetic example option. No sign-in, Health access or credentials are needed for examples. They are available to all users and never written to Health.
2. In Records, open the daily overview, trends and a record. Inspect the original waveform, zoom, full screen and two-point measurements.
3. Review candidates and segment measurements. Information buttons explain the methods, limitations and linked sources. Tap a candidate or beat to locate it in the original waveform.
4. Open the HRV information button for sources H1-H3 and the distinction from HealthKit SDNN.
5. Add a note. Generate a PDF, then use the share sheet to choose its destination. The PDF includes numbered source citations and tappable original links.
6. Settings > Health information and sources opens the complete bibliography. Usage notice, guide, data controls and privacy policy are also in Settings. The same controls and disclosures are localized into Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, German and Spanish.

HEALTH PERMISSIONS
Read access covers ECG records, heart rate and SDNN in Health on this device. HeartPro does not record ECGs; compatible Apple hardware and regional availability are required to create the original recording. An empty HealthKit query does not prove permission was granted or denied.
Write access is requested only for symptoms the user explicitly chooses to save as separate Health entries. Original ECGs are read-only. Free text, synthetic records and algorithm findings are never written to Health. Failed writes retain the local note.

PRIVACY
No accounts, ads, tracking SDKs, developer analytics or remote AI. Health records, notes and analysis are processed locally and are not automatically uploaded to us or third-party servers. CloudKit is disabled and app health storage is excluded from backups. Apple Health's own sync is controlled by the system. Sharing is user-directed. Clearing app data cannot remove Health originals or files already shared.
Support is a read-only help site, without message forms, email intake, uploads, tickets or Turnstile. Do not submit personal or health information. Cloudflare necessarily processes website delivery requests; that is disclosed separately.

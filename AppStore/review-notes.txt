HeartPro (com.alkinum.heartpro) is a native iPhone/iPad app for reviewing existing Apple Health ECGs, heart rate and HRV. No account, login, subscription, third-party tracking or remote analysis service is used. No demo credentials are required.

Review without a Watch or Health records:
1. Launch the app; read or close the usage guide.
2. Select “Try examples first” on the connection screen. All example records are explicitly synthetic, generated locally, and never written to Health.
3. The ECG tab shows history. Tap the daily overview for summaries and trends, or use the calendar to browse dates.
4. Open an ECG to inspect the waveform, zoom, two-point measurement, notes and automatic estimates. Use the expand control for full-screen viewing.
5. Add a note and select a context item. In demo mode the note stays in memory and cannot write to Health.
6. Tap the share icon to generate a PDF, then tap the share icon again to open the iOS share sheet.
7. Settings includes Help, Health access, analysis limitations, data management and a privacy-policy link.

For real records, use “Connect Apple Health” and explicitly grant the desired read access. ECG recording requires a compatible Apple Watch with ECG available in its region; the app does not record an ECG. iPad can only read records available in Health on that iPad. Empty Health read results do not prove that permission was granted.

HealthKit reads ECG, heart rate and SDNN. Optional symptom writes are requested only when the user chooses to save selected symptoms to Health. Symptoms are separate HeartPro entries; original ECGs are read-only. A denied write preserves the local note. The app never saves synthetic data or its own automated findings to Health. Clearing local data does not remove original Health records or independent symptoms already saved there.

Data handling: protected local storage, no CloudKit, no analytics/ads, no uploads of health records or notes. Exported PDFs are shared only at the user's direction. Health permission can be revoked in Apple Health; local copies and temporary reports can be cleared in HeartPro Settings.

Medical methodology and accuracy:
Apple's stored classification is source data and is distinguished from HeartPro's deterministic local algorithms. Local methods include quality checks, peak/interval estimation, segment boundaries, short-recording HRV and candidate rhythm review. No diagnoses, emergency monitoring or health-risk probabilities are provided. The rhythm reference score is a rule-based unflagged-beat fraction, not a health score. Details and references: https://heartpro.alkinum.com/docs/methods/
